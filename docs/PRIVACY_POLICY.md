# Terrastep Privacy Policy (pilot draft — requires legal review before public launch)

*Draft prepared from the Terrastep Product & Technical Plan, Sections 19–20.
Placeholders in [brackets] must be completed by the operating team.*

**Who we are.** Terrastep is operated by [team/legal entity], contact
[support email]. This policy covers the Terrastep Android app and its servers.

## What we collect, and why
- **Account data** — email address, nickname. Used to run your account and
  the game. Your nickname is your public game identity; never use your real
  name if you prefer not to.
- **Location evidence (only while you record a walk)** — GPS samples are
  collected only during a session you start, with a visible notification.
  They are used to verify walks and award territory fairly.
- **Motion data** — step counts during sessions, to cross-check movement.
- **Optional profile data** — weight (only if you provide it, used solely
  for calorie *estimates*), goals, units, language.
- **Safety data** — reports you file and accounts you block.

We do **not** collect contacts, photos (except an optional avatar you
choose), advertising IDs for tracking, or background location.

## What is public, and what is not
- Always private: your walks, routes, exact times, fitness history, weight,
  calorie estimates, and protected areas.
- Public only if you opt in to competition: your nickname, kingdom name and
  color, and the areas (H3 cells) you currently hold — shown at least
  **30 minutes after** a session ends. Public play still reveals
  location-derived information; the app explains this before you opt in.
- Never public: live location, movement direction, or a timeline of captures.

## Retention
- Raw location evidence: deleted automatically **72 hours** after upload.
- Flagged investigation evidence: up to 30 days, case-bound.
- Contribution ledger: current season + 30 days, then aggregated/unlinked.
- Private fitness summaries: until you delete them or your account.
- Security logs (no coordinates): 30 days.

## Your rights and controls
In the app you can: keep fitness private (default), pause or leave
competition at any time, set protected areas that remove cells from your
ranked play, export your data (Settings → Export), and delete your account
and data (Settings → Delete). Deletion removes your access immediately and
purges your records subject to the retention table above.

## Children
The pilot is for adults (18+). Accounts found to belong to younger users
will be removed.

## Changes
Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.

*This draft is an engineering document, not legal advice. Have qualified
